Nepal Premier League (NPL) 2026 — schedule, points table & how to follow

Season 3 overview · Last updated 30 October 2026

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is Nepal’s top franchise T20 competition. Season 3 runs from 26 October to 21 November 2026, with all matches at Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur.

This page summarises the format, current points table, recent results, upcoming fixtures and simple ways to follow live scores. Figures below reflect results through Match 6 (29 October 2026).

Format in brief

  • 8 franchise teams; each plays every other side once (7 league matches).
  • Top 4 qualify for playoffs (IPL-style): Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, Final.
  • Playoffs: 17–21 November 2026. Final on 21 November.
  • Venue: TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur (Kathmandu).
  • Typical match times: ~12:30 NPT and ~17:00 NPT on double-header days.

National team context (brief)

While the NPL runs, the national side sits around 14th in T20I and 17th in ODI rankings (ICC, late 2026). Nepal recently retained ODI status for the next cycle via Cricket World Cup League 2 results. Franchise cricket remains an important pathway for domestic players and a major fan event in Kathmandu.
