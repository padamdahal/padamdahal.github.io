What the tool is based on

The data comes from an open structured dataset derived from the Nepali Brihat Sabdakosh — the large, authoritative dictionary associated with Nepal Academy (नेपाल प्रज्ञा-प्रतिष्ठान). Earlier printed editions (starting from the 2040 B.S. series) and the later Pragya Nepali Brihat Shabdakosh (प्रज्ञा नेपाली बृहत् शब्दकोश) have long served as the standard reference for meanings, parts of speech, and word origins in Nepali.

This web version focuses on fast lookup: definitions, grammar markers (ना., वि., क्रि., etc.), etymology tags such as [सं.] for Sanskrit or [अङ्.] for English loanwords, and the ability to browse neighbouring entries.

How it compares with popular Nepali dictionaries

Nepali speakers and learners usually reach for a few well-known options depending on whether they need pure Nepali meanings, English translation, or mobile convenience.

Nepal Academy / Pragya Brihat Shabdakosh

Still the most authoritative printed (and official-app) source. The Pragya edition expanded the older Brihat Sabdakosh to well over 120,000 entries and remains the reference many writers, students, and editors treat as standard. The online tool on pbd.com.np draws from the same lineage of data, making it a convenient digital companion when you do not have the physical book or the official app open.

Nepali Shabdakosh Dictionary (Nowa Lab)

By far the most downloaded mobile dictionary for Nepali. With more than a million installs and consistently high ratings, it offers a three-way experience: Nepali→Nepali, English→Nepali, and Nepali→English. It also includes rhyming words, trending terms, and a large baby-name section. Ideal when you want an offline app that covers both languages in one place.

Other widely used options

  • Makkhay / other offline English–Nepali apps — popular for quick bilingual lookup and extra learning features such as quizzes or chat.
  • Hamro Patro’s English–Nepali dictionary — convenient if you already use the Hamro Patro ecosystem.
  • Web dictionaries such as dictionary.nepaliexpert.com or Shabdkosh’s English–Nepali section — handy for browser-only use, especially for learners who prefer English glosses.
  • Classical references — Ralph Turner’s Comparative and Etymological Dictionary of the Nepali Language (1931) remains valuable for historical and linguistic work, though it is not a modern everyday dictionary.

Each of these has a clear strength: the Academy dictionaries for authoritative Nepali definitions, the Nowa Lab app for mobile bilingual convenience, and browser tools for zero-install access. The pbd.com.np dictionary sits in the last category while staying close to the Brihat Sabdakosh tradition.

How to use the free online dictionary

  1. Type in Devanagari or Romanized Nepali. Examples: नेपाल, kiran, gyan, swatantrata.
  2. Pick from the suggestions. Autocomplete and Roman-to-Devanagari candidates appear as you type.
  3. Read the full entry. Numbered senses, part-of-speech labels, etymology, and links to nearby words are shown immediately.
  4. Optional offline use. After the letter-range data loads once, the tool continues to work without a network. A download option is also available for fuller offline storage.
Privacy note Searches never leave your browser. Only static data chunks are fetched on demand and cached locally. No accounts, no tracking of queries.

Common abbreviations you will see

  • ना. — नाम (noun)
  • वि. — विशेषण (adjective)
  • क्रि. — क्रिया (verb); also सक्रि. / अक्रि. for transitive / intransitive
  • क्रि.वि. — क्रियाविशेषण (adverb)
  • [सं.] — Sanskrit origin · [ने.] Nepali · [फा.] Persian · [अङ्.] English loanword

When to reach for which tool

  • Need the classic Nepali definition, grammar, and etymology quickly in a browser → pbd.com.np Nepali Dictionary.
  • Want a polished offline mobile app that also handles English ↔ Nepali → Nepali Shabdakosh (Nowa Lab) or similar three-way apps.
  • Prefer the official Academy edition or a physical reference → Pragya / Brihat Shabdakosh printed volumes or the Academy’s own app.
  • Focused only on English translation or learner features → dedicated English–Nepali web dictionaries or the bilingual sections of the popular apps.
