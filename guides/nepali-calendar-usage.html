How to use & embed the Interactive Nepali Calendar (BS)

Practical guide · Updated November 2026

Nepali forms, banks, and many government workflows still expect Bikram Sambat (BS) dates. Software and international documents usually want the Gregorian (AD) ISO form yyyy-mm-dd. The free Interactive Nepali Calendar on pbd.com.np bridges that gap: browse any BS month, click a day to copy the AD date, or drag a day cell onto a form field.

This page is a short how-to for people filling forms and for developers who want the same widget on their own site. It is not a second copy of the tool — the full product lives on pbd.com.np. Here you get a live embed demo, when to use it, and the minimal code to mount it yourself.

When this is useful

  • Filling Nepali forms that need AD — citizenship notes, bank KYC remarks, visa or university applications that ask for Gregorian while your papers show BS.
  • Quick “what is today in BS?” — less friction than opening a full converter for a single date.
  • Building a form for Nepali users — let them pick a BS day visually while your backend stores ISO AD.
  • Offline or low-connectivity work — after the first load, the library can run without a network.

Everyday steps (no coding)

  1. Open the calendar tool (or use the demo above).
  2. Move to the month you need.
  3. Click a day — the AD ISO date is copied; paste it where the form asks for English/Gregorian date.
  4. On a page that already embeds the calendar, drag the day cell onto an input instead of copying.
Accuracy note Coverage is roughly BS 2000–2090. Years through BS 2083 are the ones commonly verified for everyday documents. For legal or financial deadlines after that, double-check against the calendar your office or bank uses.

Embed the same calendar on your site

Load the official script once. Mount a calendar in any container. Click-to-copy and drag-to-fill then work for inputs on that page. Keep the script src pointed at pbd.com.np so users always get the maintained build.

Option A — explicit mount

<script src="https://pbd.com.np/tools/nepali-calendar/nepali-calendar.js"></script>
<div id="my-calendar"></div>
<script>
  NepaliCalendar.mount('#my-calendar', {
    lang: 'ne',
    theme: 'auto',
    timeZone: 'Asia/Kathmandu',
    showAD: true,
    langToggle: true
  });
</script>

Option B — data attribute (no mount call)

<div data-nepali-calendar
     data-lang="ne"
     data-theme="auto"
     data-time-zone="Asia/Kathmandu"></div>
<script src="https://pbd.com.np/tools/nepali-calendar/nepali-calendar.js"></script>

Optional helpers

NepaliCalendar.toAD(2082, 7, 16);   // → "2025-11-02"
NepaliCalendar.toBS("2025-11-02");  // → { year: 2082, month: 7, day: 16 }
NepaliCalendar.today("Asia/Kathmandu");
